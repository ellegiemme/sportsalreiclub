Sport Sal Rei Club was founded on 28 August 1951, with its home in Sal Rei, Boa Vista. It is one of the oldest football clubs on the island, followed by a great legion of supporters — not only in the town of Sal Rei, but in every settlement across Boa Vista.

The club’s history is rich in regional titles, with a constant presence in national competitions — crowned by the title of National Champion of Cape Verde in the 2003/2004 season.

Champions of Cape Verde

In 2003/2004, Sal Rei were crowned National Football Champions of Cape Verde. That campaign, the team also produced the championship’s best player, top scorer and best coach — as well as its best directors.

The club reached the semi-finals of the National Championship again in 2004/2005, and over the following five years remained a regular presence in the National Championship and the Cape Verde Cup, with further semi-final runs among its results.

In January 2005, Sal Rei won the Torneio dos Encarnados on the island of Sal, organised by the Santa Maria team, in a field that also included Mindelense (São Vicente) and Ultramarina (São Nicolau).

The penta years

Between 2003/2004 and 2007/2008, the club became five-times-in-a-row Regional Champions of Boa Vista — an unprecedented achievement on the island — and over the last fifteen years it has shown remarkable dynamism in sport, particularly in football, both on Boa Vista and across the other islands, taking part in numerous national championships and tournaments.

A new era

Across recent seasons the club has been ever-present in national competitions, whether as Regional Champion or as winner of the Taça Bubista — carrying with it, and honouring, the brands that have embraced the club’s successive projects.

In 2024, the current board made a landmark change with the redesign of the club’s crest. Year after year, a board made up entirely of women has been revolutionising sport on the island: winning the 2024/2025 regional title and the Taça Bubista, and fighting all the way to the final of the Cape Verde Cup that same season. In 2025/2026, the team faced Palmeira in the Cape Verde Super Cup.
