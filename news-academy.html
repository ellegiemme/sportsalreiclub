Formation School: 2026/27 enrolment is now open

July 5, 20263 min readSport Sal Rei Club
All news

Sal Rei’s Formation School has opened enrolment for the 2026/27 season. Around a hundred children and young players train with the club every week, and the school remains one of the foundations of the institution.

The Formation School is where the story of many Sal Rei players begins. Built on the idea that a football club carries a responsibility beyond the pitch, the school develops football skills and life skills side by side, across several age groups.

Football, and more than football

Training is organised so that the youngest arrivals learn through play and build a love for the game, while older groups work on technical foundations, teamwork and the discipline that carries them towards the senior squad. Throughout, coaches place as much value on respect, commitment and belonging as on results.

How to enrol

Families interested in the 2026/27 season can register their children through the club’s social headquarters in Sal Rei. Places are organised by age group, and the coaching staff will guide each new arrival to the right level.

  • Open to children and young players across several age groups
  • Weekly training led by the club’s Formation School coaches
  • Enrolment through the club’s social headquarters in Sal Rei

By investing in its academy, Sal Rei invests in Boa Vista. The club encourages families to be part of a project that has helped shape young islanders for decades — and, in this 75th anniversary season, looks ahead to many more.
