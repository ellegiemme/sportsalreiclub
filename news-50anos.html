Fifty years of the Cape Verde Championship — and Sal Rei on the roll of honour

July 8, 20263 min readSport Sal Rei Club
All news

The trophy lifted at this season’s national final carried a special mark: fifty years of the Campeonato de Cabo Verde. Among the champions inscribed in that history is Sport Sal Rei Club, winners in 2003/2004.

Half a century of national championship football in Cape Verde was marked this year with a redesigned trophy, bearing the commemoration of the competition’s fiftieth anniversary. The Federation also confirmed that prize money had been doubled this season, with awards attached to wins, draws, progression through the rounds and the title itself.

Sal Rei among the champions

For Sport Sal Rei Club, the anniversary carries a particular meaning. In 2003/2004 the club was crowned National Champion of Cape Verde — the only side from Boa Vista to have taken the title. That season, Sal Rei also produced the championship’s best player, its top scorer and its best coach.

Every year since, the competition has brought together the champions of each sporting region, from Santo Antão in the north to Brava in the south. It remains the stage on which island football measures itself against the whole country.

A story still being written

Fifty years of the national championship arrive in the same season that Sal Rei marks seventy-five years of its own. Two anniversaries, one shared history — and, for the club, a reminder that its name already belongs on that list.
