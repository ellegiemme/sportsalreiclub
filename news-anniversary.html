Sal Rei turn 75: the anniversary programme is unveiled

July 10, 20264 min readSport Sal Rei Club
All news

Founded in 1951, Sport Sal Rei Club marks three-quarters of a century this season. The club has unveiled a full programme of celebrations that will run from August through to the anniversary date, bringing together players, members and the wider Boa Vista community.

On 28 August 1951, a group of young islanders came together in Sal Rei to found what would become Boa Vista’s most decorated football club. Seventy-five years later, the red of Sal Rei is woven into the identity of the town, and the club wants the anniversary to belong to everyone who has worn, watched or supported it.

A season-long celebration

The programme opens in August with the Capitão Carlos Nascimento Tournament, the club’s traditional pre-season competition, played this year in honour of one of the great figures of Sal Rei’s history. Across the following months the club will host member gatherings, youth festivals and a series of events that look back at the people and moments that shaped the institution.

Honouring those who built the club

A central theme of the anniversary is memory. The club is gathering photographs, trophies and testimonies from former players, coaches and directors, with the aim of preserving the story of Sal Rei for the generations to come. Members are invited to bring their own memories and materials to the club’s social headquarters throughout the season.

The celebrations will also look forward. With the Formation School growing year on year and the first team competing at the top of the regional game, the 75th anniversary is being framed not as an endpoint but as a foundation for the club’s next chapter.

Key dates

  • August — Capitão Carlos Nascimento Tournament
  • Throughout the season — member gatherings and youth festivals
  • 28 August — anniversary of the club’s foundation

Full details of each event will be published on the club’s channels as the season progresses. Sal Rei invites all its members and supporters to be part of a celebration that honours the past while building the future.
