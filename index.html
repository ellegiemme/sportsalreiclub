The Club

More Than
Football

Sport Sal Rei Club was founded in Sal Rei, the capital of Boa Vista, and has grown into the island's most important sporting institution. We compete at the highest level of Cape Verdean football while maintaining our deep roots in the local community.

We believe that a football club has a responsibility that goes beyond the pitch. Through our academy, our social programmes, and our partnership with local institutions, we work every day to make Boa Vista a better place.

The Academy

Around 100 kids and young players train with us every week. Our formation school is one of the club's foundations, developing football and life skills across three age groups.

U4
First steps on the pitch, learning through play and building a love for the game.
U10
Technical foundations, teamwork and discipline as young players grow with the club.
U15
The path towards the senior squad, developing local talent into the club's future.
