Boa Vista reach the national semi-finals as África Show fall on penalties

July 6, 20264 min readSport Sal Rei Club
All news

The island of Boa Vista was represented in the semi-finals of the 2025/26 Campeonato Nacional. África Show, regional champions, won the first leg at home in Sal Rei before going out to Palmeira on penalties — the side that would go on to be crowned national champions.

Twelve teams contested this season’s Campeonato Nacional: the champions of each of the eleven sporting regions, plus an additional side, since holders Palmeira entered to defend their title.

A tie decided from the spot

África Show, champions of Boa Vista, took a 1–0 lead in the first leg of the semi-final, played in Sal Rei. Palmeira responded with a 2–1 win in the return, levelling the tie at 2–2 on aggregate, and went through 4–2 on penalties.

In the other semi-final, Mindelense — the most decorated club in Cape Verdean football — eliminated Académica do Porto Novo.

Palmeira retain the title

The final was staged on 4 July at the Estádio João Serra in Ponta do Sol, on Santo Antão — the first time the ground had hosted the decisive match. After 2–2 through normal time and extra time, Palmeira beat Mindelense 3–2 on penalties, their goalkeeper Djão saving three, to become back-to-back national champions.

It is a familiar name in Sal Rei: it was Palmeira that the club faced in the Cape Verde Super Cup this season. The road to the national stage runs through Boa Vista, and the island has shown once again that it belongs there.
