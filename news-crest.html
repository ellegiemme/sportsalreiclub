A crest for a new era: the redesign that marked a change at Sal Rei

July 4, 20263 min readSport Sal Rei Club
All news

In 2024 the club’s board made a landmark decision: to redesign the crest that Sal Rei had carried for decades. It was more than a graphic exercise — it marked the beginning of a period that has since delivered the club’s most successful seasons in years.

A football crest is rarely just a badge. It is what supporters wear, what players carry on the chest, and what a club leaves behind on every pitch it visits. Changing it is never a small decision.

The board that made the call

The change was driven by a board made up entirely of women — a leadership that has, season after season, reshaped how the club works and what it expects of itself.

What followed

The results arrived quickly. In 2024/2025 Sal Rei were crowned Regional Champions of Boa Vista and won the Taça Bubista, going on to fight all the way to the final of the Cape Verde Cup. The following season the team faced Palmeira in the Cape Verde Super Cup.

Seventy-five years after its foundation, the club carries a crest designed in its own time — and a record that has begun to match the ambition behind it.
